Specialized domain agents apply the same multi-step autonomous pattern to fields with high data volume, strict protocols, or complex matching requirements.

**Healthcare & Medical** agents address clinical workflows. A clinical trial matcher cross-references anonymized patient records against trial eligibility criteria to produce ranked recommendations. A treatment protocol monitor flags undocumented deviations from evidence-based guidelines and alerts the clinical team. A medication interaction checker validates the full drug-drug and drug-supplement interaction matrix before prescribing. An outcomes quality tracker calculates risk-adjusted metrics by provider and protocol to surface outliers.

**Research & Academia** agents reduce the overhead of scholarly work. A literature review synthesizer searches databases (PubMed, arXiv, Google Scholar), applies inclusion criteria, extracts study details, and produces narrative summaries with evidence tables and gap identification. A grant proposal builder assembles preliminary data, citation trends, and budget benchmarks into a proposal skeleton. A journal targeting assistant estimates acceptance probability by matching manuscript profiles against venue characteristics. A peer review assignment optimizer recommends reviewers by expertise match while checking for conflicts of interest.

**Education & Training** agents personalize learning at scale. A learning path generator maps diagnostic assessment results to curriculum objectives and sequences resources with checkpoint assessments. An assessment feedback agent grades submissions against rubrics and generates detailed, contextualized feedback including common misconceptions. A curriculum content generator produces materials adapted for visual, kinesthetic, and auditory learners at multiple competency levels. A student early alert agent monitors engagement and performance signals to predict risk and recommend interventions before failure.

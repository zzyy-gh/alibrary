Business operations agents automate recurring, document-heavy workflows across seven functional areas. They share a common pattern: ingest structured data, apply domain rules or frameworks, flag exceptions, and produce actionable reports.

**Legal & Compliance** agents parse contracts to flag risk clauses, build precedent summaries for legal arguments, monitor regulatory feeds for compliance drift, and extract standardized metadata from heterogeneous legal documents for discovery.

**Finance & Accounting** agents correlate transactions with supporting documents to build audit narratives, automate variance reconciliation, monitor debt covenant compliance with early warning alerts, and validate tax positions against current IRS guidance and case law.

**Human Resources** agents map skill gaps against succession needs, audit compensation equity using regression analysis, calibrate hiring panels to reduce scoring bias, and measure training ROI by linking programs to behavior change and business outcomes.

**Executive & Strategy** agents operate at the cross-functional level. A strategic intelligence agent aggregates competitor moves and internal KPIs into briefs. Situational analysis agents apply frameworks like SWOT and PESTEL (extensible to any framework). Scenario planning agents model alternative futures with contingency playbooks. Board reporting agents assemble decks from department data. OKR trackers surface what's off-track with root-cause context. M&A due diligence agents compile target profiles and assess strategic fit.

**Sales & Marketing** agents research and score prospects, attribute campaign touchpoints to revenue, optimize content calendars for brand consistency, and maintain CRM pipeline hygiene by surfacing stalled deals.

**Commerce & Supply Chain** agents optimize product catalogs, simulate pricing elasticity, forecast demand to calculate reorder points, monitor supplier performance and certifications, and reconcile inventory across warehouses to flag exceptions.

**Customer Success & Support** agents triage tickets by priority and route to the right queue, curate knowledge bases from resolved tickets, and predict customer risk at both ticket and account level to recommend retention actions.
